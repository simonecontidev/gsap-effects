About Your Stay

Steps from the subway and minutes from Midtown, our rooms are designed for the rhythm of the city: clean lines, generous light, and quiet details that let the skyline do the talking. Expect high-thread-count linens, rainfall showers, and layered acoustics that keep the buzz outside when you need to slow down.

Morning starts with locally roasted coffee and a curated print of the day’s arts and culture. Evenings end above the streets — a private view of the Empire State glow. Late checkout is standard. This is New York at your pace.
